# Review of mapped dimensional-relief cases

Reviewed August 23, 2026. This is a case-level review of the 69 mapped case
histories previously classified only as `dimensional_relief_unspecified`.

The review assigns a category only when the extracted minutes state the
requested standard, deficiency, waiver, or other direct evidence. A proposed
land use alone is not treated as evidence of requested relief. Categories are
multi-label. These findings are applied through the canonical
`resources/bza/bza_relief_case_reviews.csv` ledger; production tables and
graphics can therefore be regenerated without losing the manual review.

## Result

- 59 cases have at least one recoverable relief type.
- 10 remain unresolved because the extracted minutes omit the actual
  dimension or standard.
- The 59 recoverable cases produce 84 category assignments because 19 cases
  have more than one requested form of relief.
- Two cases identify parking relief but do not distinguish amount from layout.
- Three cases concern facade or material standards not represented in the
  current atlas taxonomy.

## Implications for the classifier

Many misses result from phrasing gaps rather than absent information. Examples
include plural lists such as “front and side yard setbacks,” bare comparisons
such as “19 spaces required; 0 proposed,” and explicit forms such as
“dimensional (height) variance.” Rules can be expanded for those constructions
while retaining exact evidence phrases.

Some misses result from damaged or incomplete extraction: several records
retain numbers but lose the name of the dimension. Those cases should remain
unresolved rather than be inferred from the proposed use.

The atlas retains `parking_supply` and `parking_layout` as detailed analytical
categories. Its primary-request display rolls those up with the two ambiguous
cases under the parent label `Parking`. `building_design_standards` is retained
as its own reviewed category.
