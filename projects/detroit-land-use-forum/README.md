# Detroit Land Use Forum contribution

This folder tracks John Bolt's exhibit and outreach contribution to the inaugural
Detroit Land Use Forum. Strong Towns Detroit is organizing the event; this project does
not duplicate event production.

## Event

- **When:** Thursday, July 30, 2026, 5:00–8:00 PM
- **Where:** VanderDetroit, Detroit, Michigan
- **Organizer:** Strong Towns Detroit
- **Purpose of this contribution:** bring a compact, substantive land-use exhibit; invite
  relevant people on short notice; learn what should become a larger multidisciplinary
  forum in 2027.

## Two tracks

### Track A — ship by July 30

The minimum successful exhibit is:

1. One visually strong, legible board explaining travel-time maps and network choke points.
2. One supporting board selected only if its content and provenance are print-ready.
3. A table surface with a small curated set of books/source material, a one-page project
   index, and a QR code/contact capture.
4. Short, personal outreach to a limited list of high-value people and institutions.
5. A simple way to record conversations, offers of help, and requests for next year.

Do not attempt to represent every Detroit land-use issue in four days.

### Track B — lead the build toward 2027

John intends to take a leading role in organizing the 2027 forum in partnership with
Strong Towns Detroit. Use the 2026 exhibit as a live prototype and relationship-building
moment for a larger, genuinely multidisciplinary forum:

- city-building as a shared mission across planning, architecture, engineering, law,
  public policy, development, finance, history, and neighborhood practice;
- public exhibition of approved community work, with explicit standards for evidence and
  presentation;
- student participation that provides portfolio-quality, supervised work rather than
  undirected volunteer labor;
- structured disagreement and exchange, not a sequence of disconnected presentations;
- published proceedings, project artifacts, contacts, and post-event commitments.

Track B begins after the July 30 retrospective. It does not compete with this week's
printing and outreach.

Taking a leading role means accepting responsibility for converting the vision into an
operable plan: governance and decision rights, budget and fundraising, venue and vendors,
institutional partnerships, program and exhibition design, public participation,
accessibility, communications, production, event-day operations, documentation, and
post-event follow-through. It does not mean acting unilaterally or displacing the existing
Strong Towns Detroit organizers.

## Working rules

- Every task has a testable outcome and evidence.
- An item is not "done" because text or a graphic exists; it is done when its final medium
  has been checked at intended size and is physically available at the venue.
- Historical maps and photographs retain source, date, creator, and rights/provenance.
- Quantitative claims show source and method in plain language.
- Outreach is invitation, not a claim that the recipient or institution endorses the event.
- External messages and purchases remain drafts/proposals until John sends or places them.
