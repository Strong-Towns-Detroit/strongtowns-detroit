## Physical kit (provisional until organizer reply)

Prefer local pickup over shipping risk.

- Mounted prints or rigid poster boards; avoid loose large-format paper unless frames/easels exist.
- Two tabletop easels or sign holders if no wall mounting is allowed.
- Neutral table covering only if the venue does not provide one.
