# Outreach queue and templates

This year's purpose is awareness and relationship opening, not maximizing raw invitation
count four days before the event.

## Priority order

1. People John already knows who are directly relevant to land use, mapping, development,
   neighborhood work, mobility, history, or taxation.
2. Named faculty, program directors, student-organization leaders, and community-engagement
   staff at Wayne State, University of Detroit Mercy, University of Michigan, and other
   southeast Michigan institutions.
3. Neighborhood and civic organizations with a specific connection to the exhibit themes.
4. Practitioners/researchers already in conversation, including Lars Doucet/Civic Mapping
   partners.
5. Generic departmental inboxes only where a named route cannot be found quickly.

For 2026, ask recipients to attend or share. Do not ask a school to create a program in
four days. Ask interested people for a short follow-up after the forum about 2027.

## Follow-up capture

For every positive reply or event conversation, record:

- what they care about;
- what they offered or requested;
- whether John promised anything;
- their preferred follow-up channel;
- the next action and a date;
- whether they may be listed publicly as interested (do not infer consent).
