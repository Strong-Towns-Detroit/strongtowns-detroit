01 · Editorial posture

Clear enough to trust.
Bold enough to remember.

Lead with the finding

Titles state what the evidence shows. A topic is not a headline.

Show the denominator

Use units, baselines, geography, time period, and uncertainty.

Annotate the evidence

Direct labels and short callouts beat detached legends and decorative copy.

Use emphasis sparingly

Yellow identifies the argument. Most data should remain navy, blue, or neutral.

Never rely on color alone

Pair color with labels, stroke styles, symbols, or patterns.

Keep provenance attached

Source, method, author, and update date travel with every graphic.
